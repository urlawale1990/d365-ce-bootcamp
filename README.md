# Dynamics 365 CE Functional Consultant Bootcamp — learner materials

Welcome. This repository holds everything you need for the bootcamp: weekly session guides, the consultant track, document templates, the Northwind Clinics client pack and the assessment criteria.

## How the course works

- **16 weeks, every weekend:** Saturday 2 hours (concept + guided lab), Sunday 2 hours (recap quiz, independent lab, consultant workshop).
- **Self-study:** plan 3–4 hours a week for reading and your Northwind capstone.
- **Two companies:** guided labs build *Contoso Home Services*; weekly capstones build *Northwind Clinics* on your own.
- **Goal:** finish job-ready for a junior Dynamics 365 CE functional consultant role, with a portfolio of 13 consultant documents and a working solution.

## Start here

1. Read [Week 0 — Onboarding](weeks/week-00-onboarding.md) and complete the checklist before Week 1.
2. Follow [Week 1](weeks/week-01-trial-setup.md) to create your Microsoft 365 work account and Dynamics 365 trials.
3. Read the [assessment criteria](assessment/README.md) so you know what you are building towards.

## Keeping your trial alive

Dynamics 365 trials last 30 days and can be extended once. A Customer Service trial expires after 14 days of no activity and cannot be reactivated. **Log in to every trial at least once a week**, and export your solution at the end of Weeks 6, 11 and 14 so a lost environment costs minutes, not weeks.
