# Week 1 lab: set up your trials and environments

The full step-by-step trial setup is in the [Week 1 guide](../weeks/week-01-trial-setup.md#trial-setup-lab). Use this sheet to check you finished it and to name your two environments, so every later lab points at the right place.

## What you should have after Week 1

| Item | Example | Where to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Microsoft 365 work account | admin@contosojane.onmicrosoft.com | [Microsoft 365 admin centre](https://admin.microsoft.com) |
| Recurring billing turned off | Business Standard trial, billing off | M365 admin centre → Billing → Your products |
| Dynamics 365 Sales trial | Sales Trial app opens with sample data | The trial link in your welcome email |
| Dynamics 365 Customer Service trial | Customer Service workspace opens | Same work account |
| Customer Insights trial (if done) | Customer Insights – Journeys opens | Same work account |

## Name your two environments

You use **two Dynamics 365 trial environments** for the whole course:

| Environment | Used for | Apps it needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Contoso** | Guided labs with the trainer | Sales and Customer Service |
| **Northwind** | Your weekly capstone, on your own | Sales and Customer Service |

1. Open the [Power Platform admin centre](https://admin.powerplatform.microsoft.com) → **Manage → Environments**.
2. Select the environment created by your **Sales** trial → **Edit** → **Name:** `Contoso` → **Save**.
3. Select your other Dynamics 365 trial environment → **Edit** → **Name:** `Northwind` → **Save**.
4. For each environment, open **Resources → Dynamics 365 apps** and check Sales and Customer Service are installed. If one is missing, select **Install app**, pick it and install it.

A plain **Trial** environment (one you created yourself with type *Trial*) can't install Dynamics 365 apps. Use the environments your Dynamics 365 trials created.
