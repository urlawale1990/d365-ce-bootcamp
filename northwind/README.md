# Northwind Clinics — client pack

Northwind Clinics is the client for your weekly capstones. Labs teach you on Contoso; capstones make you apply the same skills alone for Northwind, the way a real project works.

| File | Released | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [01-client-brief.md](01-client-brief.md) | Week 1 | Company, revenue streams, today's problems, scope, constraints |
| [02-stakeholders.md](02-stakeholders.md) | Week 2 | Who you are working with and what each person cares about |
| [week-02-call-sheet.csv](week-02-call-sheet.csv) | Week 2 | 15 interactions to log as activities |
| 03-interview-notes.md | Week 3 (after your own mock interview) | Raw stakeholder interview notes — added by your trainer |
| data/northwind-legacy-contacts.xlsx | Week 15 | 300 legacy records to clean and migrate — added by your trainer |

Files appear here as they are released. Don't look for later material early: discovering requirements yourself is part of the exercise.

**Data rule:** Northwind is fictional, but treat it like a real client. Never store medical notes in the CRM, and never put real personal data in your environment or in this repository.
