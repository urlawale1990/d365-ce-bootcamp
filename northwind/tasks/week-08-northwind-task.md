## The brief

Daniel Hughes wants quotes in minutes and the pipeline every Monday. Sam Patel, IT Manager, wants a security design he can sign off. Build the product catalogue, set a sales goal and forecast, and document the security model.

## Client material

- [Client brief](../01-client-brief.md) · [Stakeholders](../02-stakeholders.md)

## Deliver

1. A **security matrix**: business units, roles, teams and field security, with access levels per table.
2. A **product catalogue** for health packages: unit group, products in a family, a price list and at least one discount list.
3. A **sales goal** for one account manager for a quarter.
4. A **forecast** showing the quarter's pipeline by rep.
5. Screenshots of the catalogue, goal and forecast.
