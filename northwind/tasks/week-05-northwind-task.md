# Week 5 Northwind task: booking process, forms, views and a clinic manager dashboard

**Due:** Sat 12 December (see [due dates](README.md#due-dates)) · **Technique:** [Week 5 Contoso lab](../../labs/week-05-contoso-lab.md)

## The brief

Receptionists need fast screens with few clicks, on a tablet at the front desk. Map how booking works today and how it should work, then build the screens to match.

## Client material

- [Stakeholders](../02-stakeholders.md) (look at what the receptionists and Priya Shah care about)
- [As-is and to-be process map template](../../templates/04-as-is-to-be-process-map.md)

## Deliver

1. **As-is** and **to-be** process maps for a patient booking, with swimlanes.
2. A **Booking main form** whose layout follows the to-be process, plus a **quick create** form.
3. At least **3 Booking views**, each answering a real question (e.g. today's bookings at my clinic).
4. A **clinic manager dashboard** showing bookings by clinic and by status.
5. A model-driven app (or your Northwind app) that exposes Clinics, Health Packages and Bookings.
6. Screenshots of each, and the two process maps.

## Pass checks

- [ ] The form's order follows the to-be process: the most-used fields first.
- [ ] A receptionist can create a booking from the quick create form in under a minute.
- [ ] The dashboard shows bookings by clinic and status.
- [ ] Everything is in the Northwind solution.
