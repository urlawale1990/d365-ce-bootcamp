# Week 13 Northwind task: receptionist check-in app, configuration log and a change request

**Due:** Sat 13 February (see [due dates](README.md#due-dates)) · **Technique:** [Week 13 Contoso lab](../../labs/week-13-contoso-lab.md)

## The brief

The 24 receptionists work on tablets at the front desk and need few clicks. Build a check-in app for them, keep a log of everything you've configured, and handle a change request the way a real project would.

## Client material

- [Stakeholders](../02-stakeholders.md) (clinic receptionists)
- [Configuration log and change request template](../../templates/11-configuration-log-and-change-request.md)

## Deliver

1. A **canvas app** (tablet layout) for receptionists: today's bookings at a clinic, and a way to **check a patient in** that updates the booking in Dataverse.
2. The app available **inside your model-driven app** (custom page or embedded).
3. A **configuration log** listing every component you've built so far, with date and reason.
4. **One change request** with description, impact, effort, risk and decision.
5. Screenshots or a short screen recording of a check-in.

## Pass checks

- [ ] Checking a patient in writes to Dataverse (the booking status changes).
- [ ] The app opens from the model-driven app.
- [ ] The change request includes impact and effort.
- [ ] The configuration log matches what's in your solution.

## Hints

- How does the app know which clinic the receptionist is at?
- Keep the screen to what a receptionist needs in the 30 seconds a patient stands at the desk.
