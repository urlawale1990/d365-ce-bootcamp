# Week 16 Northwind task: final capstone, cutover plan and board presentation

**Due:** present on Sun 28 February (see [due dates](README.md#due-dates)) · **Technique:** [Week 16 Contoso lab](../../labs/week-16-contoso-lab.md)

## The brief

Present your Northwind solution to the “Northwind board” as if asking for approval to go live.

## Client material

- [Assessment criteria](../../assessment/README.md) · every template you've used
- [Data migration, user guide and cutover template](../../templates/13-data-migration-mapping-user-guide-and-cutover-plan.md)

## Deliver

1. A **cutover plan**: steps, owners, timings, go/no-go criteria and rollback.
2. A **go/no-go checklist** completed honestly.
3. A **15-minute presentation**:
   1. The problem in numbers (2 minutes)
   2. One story end to end, e.g. a complaint from phone call to resolved case (7 minutes)
   3. The manager's dashboard or report (2 minutes)
   4. Results against the BRD and the go-live plan (4 minutes)
4. Your **final portfolio**: all consultant documents and your exported solution.

## Pass checks

- [ ] Passes the final capstone rubric in the assessment criteria.
- [ ] The demo tells one business story, not a feature tour.
- [ ] The go/no-go checklist is honest about what isn't ready.

## Hints

- Rehearse the clicks once with your demo data loaded.
- Show your solution, its prefix and that nothing sits in Default; assessors look for it.
