# Northwind capstone: weekly task sheets

Each week you apply what you learned on Contoso to **Northwind Clinics**, on your own, in your **Northwind** environment. These sheets tell you **what** to deliver and how it will be checked, not **how** to click it: working that out is the skill. The matching Contoso lab sheet shows the techniques.

**Rules for every week**

- Build everything in your **Northwind solution** with your own publisher and prefix (from Week 4). Nothing in the Default solution.
- Never store medical notes or real personal data. Northwind is fictional; treat it like a real client.
- Hand in screenshots and documents in the class channel or your portfolio folder by the due date.
- Be demo-ready: 2–3 learners present their Northwind work each Saturday.
