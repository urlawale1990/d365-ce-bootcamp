# Week 10 Northwind task: FRD v1.0, traceability and the knowledge base

**Due:** Sat 23 January (see [due dates](README.md#due-dates)) · **Technique:** [Week 10 Contoso lab](../../labs/week-10-contoso-lab.md)

## The brief

Receptionists answer the same patient questions all day. Give them a knowledge base, and finish the functional design so every requirement can be traced from story to test.

## Client material

- Your FRD draft from Week 9 · [FRD template](../../templates/08-functional-requirements-document-frd.md)
- [Week 2 call sheet](../week-02-call-sheet.csv) (real patient questions, e.g. fasting before a blood test)

## Deliver

1. **FRD v1.0** with the service sections added.
2. A **requirements traceability matrix (RTM)**: requirement → story → design element → test.
3. **10 published knowledge articles** for common patient questions, each with keywords and a subject.
4. Proof that articles surface on a case (screenshot of knowledge search on a case).

## Pass checks

- [ ] Every story traces to a design element and a test in the RTM.
- [ ] 10 articles are published with searchable keywords.
- [ ] Opening a related case suggests the right article.
- [ ] No article contains medical advice that belongs in a clinical record.

## Hints

- Good article topics: opening hours, fasting, moving an appointment, results letters, parking, what a health check includes.
- One question per article, answered in the first sentence.
