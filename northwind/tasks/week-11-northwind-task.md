# Week 11 Northwind task: solution strategy and a health-check reminder journey

**Due:** Sat 30 January (see [due dates](README.md#due-dates)) · **Technique:** [Week 11 Contoso lab](../../labs/week-11-contoso-lab.md)

## The brief

Grace Lin, Marketing Lead, wants annual health-check reminders with proper consent records. Sam Patel wants to know how the solution will be organised and deployed. Deliver both.

## Client material

- [Stakeholders](../02-stakeholders.md) (Grace Lin, Sam Patel)
- [Solution strategy template](../../templates/09-solution-strategy.md)

## Deliver

1. A **solution strategy**: publisher and prefix, how many solutions and why, managed/unmanaged policy, environments.
2. A **segment** of patients due an annual health check, with consent.
3. A **marketing email** for the reminder, with personalisation and an unsubscribe link.
4. A **journey** that sends the reminder to the segment, with a wait and a follow-up branch.
5. Screenshots of the segment, email and journey.

## Pass checks

- [ ] The strategy names the publisher, prefix and managed/unmanaged policy, each with a reason.
- [ ] The journey sends to your segment.
- [ ] Only contacts with consent are included.
- [ ] No health details appear in the email.

## Hints

- How will you know a patient is “due” a check? What data would you need?
- What should happen if the patient doesn't open the first email?
