# Week 1 Northwind task: your Northwind environment and app recommendation

**Due:** done (see [due dates](README.md#due-dates)) · **Technique:** [Week 1 Contoso lab](../../labs/week-01-contoso-lab.md)

## The brief

Northwind's COO wants one CRM for corporate sales and patient support. Before anything is built, she needs a place to build it and a short recommendation on which Dynamics 365 apps fit.

## Client material

- [Client brief](../01-client-brief.md)

## Deliver

1. A screenshot of your **Northwind** environment in the Power Platform admin centre showing its name, type and URL.
2. A **1-page note** recommending which Dynamics 365 apps Northwind needs and why, linked to the problems in the brief.

## Pass checks

- [ ] The environment is named Northwind and opens a Dynamics 365 app.
- [ ] The note recommends Sales and Customer Service, each with a reason from the brief.
- [ ] The note says what is out of scope (e.g. clinical records).

## Hints

- Use one of the environments your Dynamics 365 trials created, renamed to Northwind. A plain *Trial* environment can't install Dynamics 365 apps.
- Tie each app to a quote from the COO, e.g. “I want to see the corporate pipeline every Monday.”
