# Northwind Clinics — client brief

**Company.** Northwind Clinics is a private healthcare group with 12 clinics across England (London 4, Manchester 3, Birmingham 2, Leeds 2, Bristol 1), 180 staff and £24m annual revenue. It offers GP appointments, health-check packages and physiotherapy.

**Two revenue streams.**

- **Individual patients (B2C):** book and pay for health checks and appointments; 40,000 bookings a year.
- **Corporate wellness (B2B):** annual contracts with employers who buy health checks for staff; 85 corporate clients, average contract £45,000, sold by a team of 6 account managers.

**Today's situation.**

- Corporate sales tracked in a shared Excel workbook; no one knows the real pipeline value.
- Patient enquiries, complaints and billing queries arrive in one shared inbox and 12 clinic phone lines; no tracking, no response targets.
- Booking data sits in a separate clinical booking system (out of scope to replace, may need integration later).
- Last year 14% of complaints took more than 10 working days to resolve; the regulator has noticed.

**What the COO wants (in her words):** “One CRM for selling to companies and looking after patients and clients. I want to see the corporate pipeline every Monday, and I want no complaint to sit unanswered for more than 2 days. Live in 6 months, on Microsoft because we already use Microsoft 365.”

**Scope (phase 1):** Dynamics 365 Sales for corporate wellness; Dynamics 365 Customer Service for patient and client support; Customer Insights – Journeys for health-check reminders; Power Platform for small apps, automation and reporting.

**Out of scope:** clinical records (patient medical notes must never be stored in the CRM), payroll, the booking system itself.

**Constraints:** UK GDPR and health data sensitivity; budget for configuration first, minimal custom code; staff have low CRM experience, so the solution must be simple.
